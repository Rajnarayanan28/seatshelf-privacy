Privacy Policy for SeatShelf

Effective Date: November 18, 2026 | Last Updated: November 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how SeatShelf (“we”, “our”, or “the app”), developed by RenderedPixelUser, collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use our mobile application.

Summary: SeatShelf is a campus seating and library companion app. We only collect the minimal account information necessary to allow you to reserve seats, create rooms, and borrow books. We do not sell your personal data, track you across other apps, or serve advertisements.

1. Information We Collect

When you use SeatShelf, we collect the following limited types of information:

  • Account Information: When you sign in using Google Sign-In, we collect your name, email address, and Google user identifier (UID) through Google Firebase Authentication to authenticate your identity.
  • App Profile & Content: We store your custom in-app nickname/handle, created space/room layouts (seat layouts and names), and seat bookings you make.
  • Library Data: If using campus library features, we track borrowed book records, loan timestamps, and return requests associated with your account.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to provide the core services of SeatShelf:

  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • To display real-time seating availability and prevent double-booking.
  • To synchronize your custom rooms, libraries, and book loans across sessions.
  • To manage librarian verification requests and library operations.

3. Third-Party Services

We use trusted industry-standard cloud providers to operate the app:

  • Google Firebase Authentication: To handle secure sign-in.
  • Google Cloud Firestore: To securely store encrypted space data, user profiles, and library databases.
  • Google Play Services: To deliver app updates and core Android framework support.

These third-party services process data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Storage and Security

We take the security of your data seriously:

  • All network communications between SeatShelf and our cloud servers are encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
  • Access to user data is governed by strict Cloud Firestore Security Rules, preventing unauthorized users from accessing or tampering with your private reservations and data.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your account and app data only as long as you maintain your account with SeatShelf. If you would like your account, spaces, or booking history permanently removed from our databases, you can contact us at any time, and we will fulfill your request promptly.

6. Children’s Privacy

SeatShelf is intended for campus students, faculty, and general space organizers. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
